### Why it's the best of the three
- 1/7 reads the plan back as a table, cross-checks total area, and says "ask me instead of guessing" — fixes the main flaw of the house set.
- 4/7 separate critic chat with a threshold ("A 7 isn't done", repeat until 9) — a real quality loop.
- 5/7 renovation preview lists load-bearing/MEP/permits — directly useful for fit-out work.
- 3/7 uses reference photos as the bar + clearance rule (3 ft) + "list what you couldn't match".

### Top weaknesses
1. Imperial units (9 ft, 5.5 ft, 3 ft, sq ft) — KSA needs metric (≈2.7–3.0 m ceiling, 1.65 m eye, 0.9 m clearance, m²).
2. Critic (4/7) assumes a matching photo for each room — unbuilt projects need renders/moodboard as reference instead.
3. No mobile/touch or performance limit anywhere; no style-switch or lighting step (fine — keeps it lean).

### Cross-set lesson
"Read back as a table + check totals + ask, don't guess" and "fresh-chat critic with a score threshold" are the two strongest patterns seen so far.
