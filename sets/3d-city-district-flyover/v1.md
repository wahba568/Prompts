# 3D City District Flyover — 7 Prompts (v1, original)

Source: Instagram @godofprompt — https://www.instagram.com/p/DfssISdDiUG/ (Nov 2026)
Setup (per post): ChatGPT with "GPT-6 Astra" + thinking on. One HTML file, open with Preview in Canvas.
Post claim: studios charge ~$50,000 for district renders like this (their own estimate, unverified).

---

## 1/7 · The Plan — Plan the district first

```
You're an urban designer. Plan a small [Mediterranean] district I'll build in 3D with Three.js. Give me: a grid map of the blocks, what sits on each one (homes with roof terraces, a plaza with a fountain, a park with a pond, a clock tower), roads with crosswalks and an elevated train loop with one station. Add sizes in meters, colors as hex codes and the 10 details that make it feel lived in.
```

## 2/7 · The Streets — Lay the ground

Ground first, buildings after.

```
Build this district in one HTML file with Three.js loaded from a CDN link. No imported 3D models or image files: make everything from shapes in code. Start with the ground: asphalt roads with white lane lines and crosswalks, sidewalks with curbs, grass areas and the stone plaza. Soft sunlight with long shadows and a pale sky. Add orbit controls so I can drag, zoom and fly around.
```

## 3/7 · The Homes — Add the buildings

One kind of object per message. That keeps every detail sharp.

```
Now add the homes from the plan. White buildings, 2 to 4 floors, with tall windows, small balconies with railings and flat roofs with terraces, plants and a water tank. Warm light glowing in some windows. Vary the height and layout so no two look the same. Keep everything already built exactly as it is. Then tell me which part of the scene looks the least real.
```

## 4/7 · The Life — Fill the plaza and park

```
Fill the plaza and park: a round fountain inside a ring of hedges, café tables with chairs and white umbrellas, benches, street lamps, potted trees, palm trees along the roads, and a pond with rocks and a wooden gazebo lit warm inside. Simple shapes, real proportions. Spread things naturally, not in perfect rows. Keep the rest of the city the same.
```

## 5/7 · The Train — Add the wow piece

```
Add an elevated train loop around the district on white concrete pillars, with rails and sleepers on top. Add one station with a curved glass and steel roof, a platform and stairs down to the street. Add a small 2-car train that runs around the loop slowly and stops at the station for 3 seconds. Keep it smooth on a normal laptop.
```

## 6/7 · The Flyover — Film the camera tour

Open full screen and screen-record it. This is the video you post and send to clients.

```
Add a "Tour" button. When I press it, hide all buttons and fly the camera through the district in 20 seconds: start wide from above, swoop down to the fountain, glide past the café tables, follow the train into the station, then rise back to the wide view. Smooth easing, no sudden turns. When it ends, loop back to the start.
```

## 7/7 · The Client — Sell it to a developer

Post: a small developer will pay a few thousand for a 3D preview (their claim).

```
Write a DM to [the sales manager] at [developer], who is selling [project name], a new [housing district] in [city]. I made a free 3D flyover of a district like theirs: [link]. Open with what they get: buyers walk the finished streets before anything is built. Offer: a 3D tour of their own project from their plans for [your price], ready in 10 days. Under 70 words.
```
