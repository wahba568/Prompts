# Floor Plan → 3D Walkthrough (with Harsh Critic) — 7 Prompts (v1, original)

Source: Instagram @godofprompt, posted 23 Oct 2026 — https://www.instagram.com/p/Dez6Y_ijuhG/
Caption hook: "Someone gave Claude a photo of a floor plan. 40 minutes later he had a 3D walkthrough…"
Slides 1, 2 and 11 not captured (cover + CTA). Imperial units as in original.

Who it's for (slide 3):
- Realtors → tours for homes that don't exist yet
- Builders → a tour for every plan in your catalog
- Interior designers → show the room before you buy a thing
- Homeowners → see the reno before you pay for it
- Freelancers → sell this as a service

---

## 1/7 · Accuracy — The Blueprint Read
Make Claude read the plan back to you before it builds anything. That's where the accuracy comes from.

```
Here's a floor plan. Before you build anything, read it back to me as a table: every room, its size, its doors and windows, and which rooms it connects to.
Use the dimensions printed on the plan. Add up the square footage and check it against the total on the plan.
If something is unclear, ask me instead of guessing.
```

## 2/7 · The Build — The Walkthrough
One file. Opens in any browser. You walk it like a video game.

```
Now build it as a single-file three.js scene I can walk through in first person.
Keep it to scale: 9 ft ceilings, eye level at 5.5 ft, walls I can't walk through.
WASD to move, click and drag to look around.
Doorways stay open, windows let the light in.
Show the room name when I walk in, and put a minimap with a 'you are here' dot in the corner.
```

## 3/7 · Design — The Interior Designer
An empty box doesn't sell. A furnished home does.

```
Furnish every room in [modern farmhouse] style.
Match these photos for the floors, cabinets, lighting and colors: [attach photos]. The photos are the bar.
Use real furniture sizes and leave at least 3 ft to walk around everything.
Warm daylight through the windows.
Then list anything you couldn't match.
```

## 4/7 · Quality Check — The Harsh Critic
The trick from the original post: one Claude builds, a fresh one judges. Open a new chat for this.

```
You're a strict critic. You didn't build this.
I'll send you a screenshot of each room next to the photo it should match.
For each pair: score it from 1 to 10, name the single biggest gap, and tell me exactly what to change.
A 7 isn't done.
```
Paste the notes back into the build chat. Repeat until every room is a 9.

## 5/7 · Renovation — The Reno Preview
See the change before you pay a contractor.

```
Make a copy of the house with these changes: [remove the wall between the kitchen and living room, add an island].
Add a button that flips between before and after, and highlight what changed.
Then list what this change involves (load-bearing walls, plumbing, electrical, permits) so I know what to ask my contractor.
```

## 6/7 · Real Estate — The Listing Tour
For realtors: a tour buyers don't have to drive to.

```
Add a guided tour mode.
The camera glides through the house in the order a buyer would walk it, about 90 seconds in total.
Show a one-line caption in each room, written like a listing: what the room is great for, not just its size.
Add a Start Tour button.
```

## 7/7 · Make Money — The $5K Pitch
The play from the original post: build one tour for free, then sell the rest of the catalog.

```
Write a cold email to [builder name]. They have floor plans on their site but no 3D tours. I already built one from their [plan name] plan: [link].
Subject line under 6 words. Body under 120 words.
Lead with the link, then offer to do the rest of their catalog at [your price] per plan.
No hype words, no 'I hope this finds you well.'
```
